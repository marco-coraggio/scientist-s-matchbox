# Template for structured text files
------------------------------------
* Marco Coraggio | v1.0 | Part of the Scientist's Matchbox

Don't underestimate the humble text files. They are great for simple unformatted documents, as they are quick, readable, and portable. They just need a few rules to format structure.


## Lists
--------

Bulleted lists:

- one
- two
  -- two.one (two spaces for indentation)
  -- two.two
    --- two.two.one
	--- two.two.three
- three

Numbered lists:
	
1. First
2. Second
  2.1. Second.First
  2.2. Second.Second
    2.2.1 Second.Second.First
    2.2.2 Second.Second.Second
3. Third

Spacing rules:

- 0 blank lines before the title.
- 2 blank lines before section headers.
- 1 blank line before paragraphs.
- 1 blank line berfore lists.


## Example of structure
------------------------

	# Title
	-------
	* Authorship/dates/versioning

	A paragraph.


	## Section header
	-----------------

	A paragraph.


	### Subsection header
	---------------------

	A paragraph.


	### Subsubsection header
	------------------------

	A paragraph.


	## With very long section headers keep underline capped at 50 characters
	--------------------------------------------------
	
	A paragraph.
